INCOBES / MEMBER SPACE

A private space for collaboration.

The member space lets authorized members manage their profile, browse the directory and collaborate within INCOBES.

01 / FEATURES

What it does

The private portal brings together profiles, skills, exchanges, proposals and collaboration requests. Each person manages their profile and chooses whether some contact details are shared with active members.

Members can submit a profile photo or institutional logo. An administrator reviews the files; if approved, they appear only in the internal directory. They are not automatically published on the public website.

02 / GOOGLE DRIVE

How Google data is used

A member-space administrator connects Google Drive using incobes@gmail.com. The app requests identity and email to confirm that account, and the drive.file permission to create and use folders and files associated with portal uploads.

The app does not request general access to all of Drive, and does not use Google data for ads, data sales or model training. Members do not connect their own Google accounts to upload photos.
