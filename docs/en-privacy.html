Privacy notice

Information about the public website, private member space and Google Drive use.

Draft for final review

The responsible party’s details have been added. Before publication, check that the practices described match how the website and member space currently operate.

Draft updated November 18, 2026. Update this date when the notice is published.

1. Responsible party and contact

Privacy and data-rights contact: incobes@gmail.com.

2. Personal data we process

  • Account and access: name, sign-in email, role, account status and administrative action records.
  • Profile: institution, country, disciplines, introduction, availability, skills, professional links and visibility preferences. Phone and alternate email are optional.
  • Portal activity: exchanges, proposals, resource listings, collaboration requests and responses.
  • Images: profile photo and institutional logo a member chooses to submit, plus review status and consent record.
  • Public website information: name, affiliation, biography, photo, professional contact, links and publications approved for display.
  • Membership applications sent by email: information included in a letter of intent and CV or academic summary.
  • Access and security: the sign-in provider may process the authorized email, IP address and technical data needed to secure access.
  • Administrator Google Drive: authorized account email, application folder/file identifiers and metadata, and an encrypted refresh token.

The portal does not intentionally request sensitive personal data. Do not add clinical, biometric or financial information, or research-participant identifiers, to profiles, proposals or resources.

3. Purposes

We use data to manage accounts and permissions; maintain the private member directory; support exchanges, proposals and collaboration requests; manage links and resources; review images submitted by members; handle membership applications received by email; and protect portal operations and security.

Photos and logos are optional. If approved, they are shown only to active members in the private directory. Saving or approving an image does not publish it on the public INCOBES website; public publication follows a separate process.

4. Google OAuth and Google Drive

An administrator connects Drive using incobes@gmail.com. The OAuth flow requests openid, email and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. The identity and email confirm the authorized account. Drive is used to create private folders and store profile photos and institutional logos submitted through the portal.

The drive.file permission is used for files and folders created by or associated with this application; the portal does not request general access to all of Drive. The refresh token is encrypted with AES-GCM and stored in D1; the key and OAuth credentials are held as Cloudflare Worker secrets. Image bytes are stored in INCOBES’s Drive, and the portal serves an approved image only to a person with active access.

Google data are not sold, used for advertising or used to train generalized AI models. They are used only to operate this upload and review feature, consistent with Google’s Limited Use requirements.

5. Service providers and recipients

Cloudflare provides Worker hosting, email-based access control and the D1 database. Google provides OAuth and Drive image storage. GitHub Pages hosts the public website, which is managed separately and does not automatically receive private profiles or portal uploads. These providers may process technical information where their services operate and apply their own privacy and security terms.

The portal does not sell personal data or provide it to third parties for advertising. External links are governed by the privacy practices of their respective providers.

6. Visibility and retention

Member-space data are shown according to portal permissions and profile visibility settings. Suspending an account blocks access but does not automatically erase its records. When an image is replaced, the previous version may remain in Drive as a record until it is manually deleted.

We retain information as needed to manage participation, review membership applications and respond to privacy requests. Messages and attachments received by email remain in the network mailbox while needed for those purposes. To request deletion, including an older Drive image, email incobes@gmail.com. We will review the request and, where appropriate, remove data from D1 and Drive. Information published on the open website may have been copied by third parties.

7. Security

The portal requires an authorized email and an active account. It applies role-based access controls, HTTPS transport and AES-GCM encryption for Google’s refresh token. Photos and logos are served from Drive only after checking access and approval status. No internet-connected system can guarantee zero risk.

8. Data rights and consent withdrawal

You may request access, correction, deletion or objection, limit use or disclosure, and withdraw optional consent. Email incobes@gmail.com with the subject Personal data request, the email associated with your account, the right you wish to exercise and a clear description of the data. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity. Do not email sensitive documents unless secure instructions are provided.

You can also edit available fields in “My profile” and ask an administrator to remove an image. Deletion may be limited by legal obligations or the need to address pending responsibilities.

9. Changes to this notice

Changes will be posted at this same address with an updated review date. If a change affects the purposes or manner of processing, members will be notified through an available channel.
